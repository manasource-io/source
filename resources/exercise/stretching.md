Mobility and flexibility work is the type covering practice that takes joints through their available range of motion. Static stretching (holding a position for 20–60 seconds), dynamic stretching (moving through a range rhythmically), and related mobility drills are techniques within this type rather than separate evidence resources.

This resource publishes no type-level claims. The literature this corpus has reviewed so far reports results for specific techniques, timings, and populations — for example the effect of pre-exercise static stretching on force output — and none of it was found to license a claim about mobility and flexibility work as a class. Rather than restate technique-level findings as type-level evidence, the resource carries an empty claim set and a score of 0 until type-level evidence is curated.
